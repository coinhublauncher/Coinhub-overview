### How CoinHub Works

#### 1. Trading generates fees

When users trade supported tokens on Zora or Clanker, trading fees are generated at the protocol level.

These fees are automatically split:
- Part in the traded token
- Part in ETH

Zora handles this automatically and pays out to the dev wallet.

---

#### 2. Fee tokens go to the treasury

All tokens received as fees are moved into the CoinHub treasury.

These tokens:
- Are held transparently
- Are not sold back into the market
- Represent long term alignment with the ecosystem

The treasury is a custody and governance layer only. It does not trade.

---

#### 3. ETH fees are split between growth and sustainability

ETH fees are split evenly into two paths:
- One half is retained as protocol revenue, held in the treasury
- One half is used to buy CoinHub's own token on the open market

This creates a direct link between usage and value flow.

---

#### 4. Buybacks strengthen the protocol

The ETH allocated for buybacks is used to purchase the CoinHub token on supported decentralized exchanges.

All purchased CoinHub tokens are then sent directly to the treasury.

This means:
- Buy pressure is driven by real usage
- Tokens are removed from circulation and held long term
- No new tokens are created to fund growth

---

#### 5. Everything is onchain and verifiable

Every step of the process happens onchain:
- Fee collection
- Token transfers
- Buybacks
- Treasury custody

Anyone can verify the flow independently.

---

### The Result

Trading activity creates fees.
Fees fund buybacks and long term treasury assets.
Value accrues from real usage, not speculation.
